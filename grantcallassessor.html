Grant Call Assessor

AI-assisted funding opportunity assessment

A prototype that combines a PI profile with current funding opportunities to identify relevant calls and produce a focused assessment.

The starting point

Researchers often have to work through lengthy and inconsistent funding call information to judge whether an opportunity is genuinely relevant. A professor approached me about whether AI could help make that initial assessment faster and more consistent, particularly around eligibility, key requirements and research fit.

The problem

Researchers and research support teams can spend significant time scanning funding opportunities that may not fit a particular PI’s expertise, career stage, eligibility or strategic priorities. The relevant information is spread across profile pages, funder listings and detailed call documents.

What I developed

I developed Grant Call Assessor as a prototype workflow for turning that search into a structured assessment. It combines a PI profile with current UKRI opportunities, applies deterministic triage rules, enriches shortlisted calls with fuller detail, and then uses Gemini for more nuanced fit assessment and proposal ideas.

How it works

  1. A PI profile URL is submitted to the application.
  2. The PI profile is converted into structured data, or reused if already available.
  3. Current UKRI opportunities are scraped and cached.
  4. Explicit rules filter and prioritise the opportunities.
  5. Full call details are extracted for the shortlist.
  6. Optional AI stages assess fit and generate proposal concepts.
  7. The workflow produces structured reports and exportable artefacts.

Deterministic and AI-assisted

The first stage applies explicit triage rules to the opportunity listings and PI profile. It checks status, eligibility, funder and subject-area signals, career-stage fit and overlap with the PI’s research interests, then identifies which calls merit fuller review. The rules can also identify obvious career-stage mismatches, such as independence or career-restart schemes aimed at earlier-career researchers. AI is used afterwards for more nuanced assessment and proposal concept generation.

Prototype scope

The current prototype has one live end-to-end funder pipeline: UKRI. The public demonstration uses a synthetic Alex Morgan profile and is intended to show the workflow rather than represent a production service.

Development approach

I defined the workflow, data structures, rules and report outputs, then developed and refined the application iteratively with extensive AI assistance during implementation.

Technology

Python Web scraping UKRI data Rule-based triage Gemini API Structured reports
