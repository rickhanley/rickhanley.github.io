Grant Call Assessor

AI-assisted funding opportunity assessment

A prototype that combines a PI profile with current funding opportunities to identify relevant calls and produce a focused assessment.

The starting point

The project grew from a real operational need. A professor approached me about whether AI could help researchers assess funding calls more quickly and consistently, particularly when identifying eligibility, key requirements and fit.

The problem

Researchers and research support teams can spend significant time scanning funding opportunities that may not fit a particular PI’s expertise, career stage, eligibility or strategic priorities. The relevant information is spread across profile pages, funder listings and detailed call documents.

What I developed

I developed Grant Call Assessor as a prototype workflow for turning that search into a more structured assessment. It takes a PI profile URL, identifies current UKRI opportunities, applies deterministic triage rules, enriches shortlisted calls and uses Gemini for higher-level fit assessment and proposal ideas.

How it works

  1. A PI profile URL is submitted to the application.
  2. The profile is generated or reused as structured data.
  3. Current UKRI opportunities are scraped and cached.
  4. Explicit rules filter and prioritise the opportunities.
  5. Full call details are extracted for the shortlist.
  6. Optional AI stages assess fit and draft proposal ideas.
  7. The workflow produces structured reports and exportable artefacts.

Deterministic and AI-assisted

The first pass is rules-based, so the filtering remains explicit and inspectable. AI is used later for nuanced assessment and drafting, where a richer interpretation of the shortlisted information is useful.

Prototype scope

The current prototype has one live end-to-end funder pipeline: UKRI. The public demonstration uses a synthetic Alex Morgan profile and is intended to show the workflow rather than represent a production service.

Development approach

I defined the workflow, data structures, rules and report outputs, then developed and refined the application iteratively with extensive AI assistance during implementation.

Technology

Python Web scraping UKRI data Rule-based triage Gemini API Structured reports
